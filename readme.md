# Personal Finance Management Prototype

## Problem Statement

Seven out of ten young adults, women, and underserved groups lack the basic knowledge needed to budget, save, or understand credit. This gap is one that personal finance education in schools has failed to close. The gap is measurable and gendered: men answered 53% of financial literacy questions correctly in 2025, compared with 45% for women, according to the TIAA Institute-GFLEC Personal Finance Index. The consequences are greatest for those with the least knowledge—adults with very low financial literacy are twice as likely to be debt-constrained and three times more likely to be financially fragile.

## Solution Summary

This prototype helps users manage their finances by:

- Entering their monthly salary
- Dividing their salary into spending and savings
- Estimating how long it may take to reach a specific financial goal
