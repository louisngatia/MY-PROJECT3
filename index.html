70 out of 10 young adults, women, and underserved groups lack the basic knowledge to budget, save, or understand credit — a gap that personal finance education in schools has failed to close. The gap is measurable and gendered: men answered 53% of financial literacy questions correctly in 2025, versus 45% for women, per the TIAA Institute-GFLEC Personal Finance Index. The consequences fall hardest on those with the least knowledge — adults with very low financial literacy are twice as likely to be debt-constrained and three times more likely to be financially fragile.

Why this matters

In every group of five one young person is financially illterate and yet most of them do not go for financial training but they opt to take loans to solve problems.
